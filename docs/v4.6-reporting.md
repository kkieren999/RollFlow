# V4.6 Leadership dashboards and reporting — staging

Protected /live/reports/ provides four aggregate domains. Each report is selected by exact module and scope from currently valid grants; no job-title or administrator bypass, no cross-grant scope borrowing. Principal/deputy may be assigned school scope, HOD faculty (verified regular staff allocation), HOY year (all reviewed class years and current roster identities/year agree). Workforce year scope omits personal absence/duty metrics. Educational support school scope only; fixed completed Monday–Sunday week, distinct student counts, under five including zero suppressed; no category/state/student breakdown or totals. This is basic minimisation, not full statistical disclosure control. No student-level or staff-identifying exports.

Date filters up to seven days within past year. Current source-ready publication only; out-of-calendar days excluded; archived history not reconstructed. Attendance counts scheduled valid keys, verified submissions, outstanding, overdue, scheduled exceptions and current revision manual attestation, no official attendance percentages. Movement departure-date cohort with current states, no inferred location. Workforce uses current cover validity (not historical cover proof), approved duties, room changes and closures. Support uses current identity/enrolment and date of request; no medical or case narratives.

Grant revocation and expiry rechecked; POST CSRF, exact digest and confirmation, audited export metadata, no student identifiers. No database migration needed; existing grants never expanded. No print/export of raw student records.
