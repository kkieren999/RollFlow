# RC3 staff onboarding and training — fictional rehearsal

Use the separate offline fictional training demo first, then protected local staging with individually issued fictional credentials. Do not use actual names, student IDs, health/support details, departmental passwords or real OneSchool exports. Role names alone do not grant access. Training facilitators should confirm intended scope and negative tests with a separate account.

| Module | Who practises | Rehearsal and expected outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Sign in and navigation | All roles | Open own permitted workspace; switch theme, keyboard navigate, sign out. No unexpected modules or other staff notices. |
| Daily schedule / attendance | Teachers and relief | Find verified lesson, save draft, mark all and certify when allowed. Submitted roll locks. Draft is not official attendance. |
| Student profile / movement | Teachers, student services | Use authorised student profile; issue pass in current lesson, receiver confirms arrival/release, issuer confirms return. A pass is not a live location or attendance mark. |
| Attendance review | Attendance officer, administrator | Review central status; administrator reviews documented correction and local reconciliation. OneSchool remains authoritative; local export is not delivery. |
| Workforce | Operations and relief | Inspect dated approved cover, room changes and personal inbox. Acknowledge own notice; reject unrelated access. |
| Learning support | Teaching, learning support and guidance roles as explicitly granted | Structured educational tasks/referrals only; no safeguarding, clinical or personal notes. Follow school urgent incident procedures outside RollFlow. |
| Leadership | Explicit report holders | Select authorised aggregate scope and dates; verify no ungranted export or named records. |
| Administration | Administrators | Review pending accounts, grants, source readiness and audit; do not change gate policy or assign blanket permissions. |

## Facilitator checklist
- Use a fictional scenario for each actual role represented; leadership, HOD, year-level coordination, business/office, teacher, relief, aide, learning support and guidance may have different explicitly assigned scopes. Test all 13 fixture roles as well as the school's proposed role matrix before acceptance.
- Ask staff to demonstrate their normal workflow unaided, a stale/conflict response and the approved manual fallback. Include keyboard, zoom, screen-reader and small-screen tests. Record accessibility barriers and feedback without personal records.
- For an unavailable system or questionable record, stop relying on it; inform the nominated school operations contact and use the approved existing attendance/incident process. Never mark an assumed student location or duplicate a pending submission.
- Record completion and unresolved training needs in the private acceptance record, not in this public repository. Training completion is not production authorisation.
