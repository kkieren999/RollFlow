# V4.0 Accounts, Roles and Security — staging

Additive migration 0016 creates RoleTemplate and AccessAudit, adds optional starts_at/expires_at and source-template provenance to existing access grants. No existing grant is widened. Existing grants retain their prior scope and no scheduled expiry. Templates are reviewed by a full administrator, copied on assignment, and archived rather than edited; existing copies require explicit revocation. All capabilities currently delegated are read-only or attendance summary. Class scope exposes only published class/timetable metadata, not rosters or attendance marking. Faculty/year logic remains isolated.

Administrators can create/archive templates, assign time-bounded grants, revoke, inspect effective access, and inspect structured audit history. Account deactivation revokes scoped grants and sessions; reactivation requires reviewed replacement grants. Admin account mutation paths lock administrators in deterministic order and recheck the actor; protected raw Django User/Profile/Group edits are blocked. Staff can change their own password with current password and validation; other sessions become invalid. Existing password hashing, throttling, TLS/host settings and CSRF remain. Pending accounts cannot use teaching or administrator routes.

Suggested bundles: Principal/Deputy school review; HOD faculty; Head of Year year; Attendance Officer school summary; other staff only the capabilities approved for their duties. Sensitive guidance, medical, support, staff operations, relief marking, class/student-specific support rights and delegated write/export permissions are not implemented. No SSO/MFA until an approved identity provider and configuration exist. No automatic production provisioning. Audit is application-append-only, not tamper-proof against DB operators; retention policy and independent review remain pending. Temporary class grants are metadata-only; exact timed relief lesson assignment and relief attendance ownership remain to be built. Production approvals and V3.9 Windows validation still required.
